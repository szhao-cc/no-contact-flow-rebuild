# Growth Audit — No Contact

Five ranked ideas plus a clickable prototype of No Contact: Breakup Recovery's onboarding and paywall, rebuilt in its own design.

**Live:** https://szhao-cc.github.io/no-contact-flow-rebuild/ · on a phone: https://szhao-cc.github.io/no-contact-flow-rebuild/?open=proto

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Offer a smaller first step when someone closes the paywall — Monthly Access at $12.99 with your own "Everything you get" table, no trial, no timer | ARPU +10–15% |
| 02 | Put their goal in the paywall headline — "Your 30 days start tonight." | CR +15–20% |
| 03 | Make the plan date match the goal they set, and let the loader name their answers | CR +10–15% |
| 04 | Show your 4.7 stars from 2,391 App Store ratings, and one review, where people pay | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Ask for notifications after the purchase, not in three steps before the prices | CR +10–15% |

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for No Contact.

## Grounding

Every price, rating, quote and feature line comes from No Contact's own App Store listing (US) and its in-app screens: the in-app purchase list (Quarterly $34.99, Weekly $6.99, Monthly $12.99), the 4.7 rating from 2,391 ratings, the 3-day trial shown on the paywall, the "Everything you get" table and the reviews shown in onboarding.

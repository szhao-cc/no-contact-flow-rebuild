What this is

We went through No Contact the way someone after a breakup does, then rebuilt the parts we think are leaving money on the table.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we get to see what actually moves conversion and ARPU across the market rather than inside one app. We pointed that at No Contact: installed it, answered every question up to the paywall and past it, read your App Store listing and your in-app purchase list, and looked for gaps between what you already have and what you currently show people.

Your flow is long and careful, and most of it works. What follows is five ideas — not fifteen. Each one is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and each one is built into a clickable prototype so you can see it rather than imagine it. The prototype uses your design, not ours.

We did not redesign your app

Every screen in the prototype is yours — the lavender gradient, the frosted option cards, the serif questions, Kai, the coloured loader bars and the mauve trial-timeline paywall. Only the order changed, plus a few things you already own but do not show at the right moment. This is not a design pitch.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If one of these is something you have already run, tell us — we will go deeper on the rest rather than repeat it.
